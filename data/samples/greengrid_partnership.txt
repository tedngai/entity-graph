GreenGrid Energy Signs Strategic Partnership with Nordwind Capital

BERLIN — GreenGrid Energy, the Berlin-based renewable infrastructure company
founded by Dr. Maria Chen in 2019, announced a strategic partnership with
Nordwind Capital, the Hamburg investment firm led by Tobias Renner.

Under the agreement, Nordwind Capital will invest 400 million euros in
GreenGrid Energy's offshore wind portfolio across the Baltic Sea. Maria Chen
said the partnership will accelerate the company's expansion into Poland and
Denmark, where GreenGrid Energy plans to build three new wind farms.

Tobias Renner, who previously worked at the European Investment Bank,
described the deal as a long-term commitment to the energy transition.
"Our analysis shows that offshore wind in the Baltic region can deliver
stable returns for decades," Renner said at a press conference in Berlin.

The European Commission approved the transaction last week after a
competition review. GreenGrid Energy will cooperate with the University of
Rostock on research into grid storage, and Dr. Maria Chen joins the
university's energy advisory board.

Nordwind Capital, founded in 2011, manages roughly 12 billion euros and has
offices in Hamburg, London, and Singapore.
